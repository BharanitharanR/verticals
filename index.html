VERTICALS / 001 · WHAT IS IT

Your business, answered on WhatsApp.

Verticals runs on your own machine, under your own WhatsApp number. You decide what customers see and how the conversation goes — in plain English, no code. It handles the talking, and quietly runs the numbers behind the scenes.

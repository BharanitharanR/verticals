Audio Book Junkie

SUMMON PHRASE

@abj

WHAT YOU CAN ASK

  • @abj read me Crime and Punishment
  • @abj read me the next page
  • @abj prescribe me a book
  • @abj what books do you have

Any message to ABJ has to include "@abj" somewhere in it, or it won't respond - that's how it tells your message apart from everyone else's on the same WhatsApp number. If it doesn't already have a book you ask for, it checks Project Gutenberg for a public-domain copy before giving up.
